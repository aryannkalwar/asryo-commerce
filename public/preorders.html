Before pre-orders open

Each eligible product must display its final price, available sizes, quantity limit, estimated dispatch date, expected delivery window, shipping cost, and cancellation/refund terms before an order is accepted. Delays must be communicated with a revised estimate and a cancellation option. We will not describe unmanufactured pieces as ready to ship.

Customer protections for the future pre-order launch

Pre-orders will require clear affirmative consent to the stated dispatch estimate and terms. Customers must be able to request cancellation before dispatch, with any applicable refund processed to the original payment method in accordance with published terms and Indian consumer law. An order reference and support contact will be supplied. If the stated dispatch estimate cannot be met, ASRYO will notify the customer and offer a cancellation/refund route.

Support contact details and exact fulfilment dates will be published before checkout opens.
