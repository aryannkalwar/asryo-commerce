BEFORE WE OPEN

Service, made clear.

Orders are not open while we confirm ASRYO’s products, fulfilment and customer support details. These pages are a preview and are not final legal policies.

Ordering & payment

Orders are currently closed. The planned checkout is cash on delivery only; no online card or bank payment will be collected. Final prices, stock, payment terms and delivery regions will be confirmed before sales begin.

Shipping, returns & cancellations

Shipping charges, delivery timelines, return eligibility, cancellation terms and the return address are being confirmed. No order should be accepted until these details are published.

Privacy

When customer accounts and ordering are enabled, ASRYO will use account and order details to provide account access, fulfil orders and support customers. No card or bank details are requested or stored. When the AI assistant is enabled, its notice explains that a question is sent to OpenAI for a reply. The current preview does not replace the full privacy notice required before customer accounts and ordering open.

Drop 001 early-access emails

Email signup requires an explicit consent checkbox and confirmation through a link sent to your email address. Unconfirmed addresses are not included in the active launch list. Once enabled, ASRYO will store your email address, date of consent and date of verification for the limited purpose of sending updates about Drop 001. Confirmation emails are delivered through ASRYO's configured email provider. No launch-update messages are sent until you verify the address. Every confirmation message contains an unsubscribe link. You may unsubscribe at any time; your address is excluded from the active list, although a suppression record is retained. This launch signup is not yet active while the email sender is being configured.

Size guide

Product concept sizes and measurements are not final. To check your preferred fit, lay an existing T-shirt flat, measure chest width, body length, shoulder width and sleeve length, and compare these with the final ASRYO chart once sampling is complete.
