Ordering & payment

ASRYO accepts cash on delivery only. No online payment is collected. Pay the order total in cash when the delivery arrives. Product prices, stock, eligible delivery regions and final order terms will be confirmed before ordering opens.

Shipping, returns & cancellations

Shipping charges, delivery timelines, return eligibility, cancellation terms and the return address are being confirmed. No order should be accepted until these details are published.

Privacy

When customer accounts and ordering are enabled, ASRYO will use account and order details to provide account access, fulfil orders and support customers. No card or bank details are requested or stored. When the AI assistant is enabled, its notice explains that a question is sent to OpenAI for a reply. This preview does not replace a complete privacy notice or the final business contact details.
