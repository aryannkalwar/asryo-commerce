02 / THE DETAIL

DESIGN FIRST / QUALITY ALWAYS

DETAILS MATTER.

We are developing the first ASRYO garments through a sample-first process. Specifications below describe the direction, not yet confirmed production claims.

01

THE SILHOUETTE

Oversized cuts and a considered drop-shoulder fit are the starting point. Final garment measurements will follow physical fit testing.

02

THE FABRIC

Heavyweight cotton is the target feel. Final fibre composition, GSM and wash performance remain subject to sample verification.

03

THE GRAPHICS

Original artwork, placement, colour and print texture are evaluated before manufacturing. Current visuals are design concepts.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

THE DETAILS.

Is ASRYO taking orders yet?

Not yet. These are original design concepts. Orders will open only after physical sampling, confirmed stock, pricing, fulfilment and customer-support details.

Are the fabric specifications final?

No. Heavyweight oversized cotton is the direction; fabric composition, GSM, garment sizes, print finish and wash tests are still being verified.

Will early access send me marketing emails?

Your interest is recorded only with consent. Until email verification is connected, it is not an active newsletter subscription. You receive a private cancellation link when you submit interest.

Where can I view more designs?

The ASRYO Design Archive contains the extended concept collection, including hoodies, tracksuits and accessories.
