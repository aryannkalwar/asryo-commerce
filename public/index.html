YOUR QUESTIONS.

Launch information, with no promises before the details are confirmed.

Can I buy these T-shirts now?

Not yet. All T-shirts are design previews, and the store is not accepting orders.

What fabric and sizes will be available?

Fabric weight, final material, size options and garment measurements depend on physical samples and manufacturer confirmation. Each design page explains how to measure a T-shirt you own.

How much will shipping and returns cost?

Charges, delivery regions, dispatch times and return or cancellation rules will be published before ASRYO begins accepting orders. See pre-launch policies.

How does early access work?

Once a verified sender is configured, you can request a confirmation email. Only confirmed addresses will be kept on the active launch list. You may unsubscribe using the link in the email.
